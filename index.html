About Me

I am a Computer Science graduate from the Technological Institute of the Philippines in Manila, and I love building at the intersection of AI, Data, and Cloud computing. My journey started with training computer vision models for an autonomous RC car, which sparked my curiosity about how data is actually handled behind the scenes. That curiosity led me through a Business Analyst internship where I coded Python scripts to normalize records, and eventually to a Data Engineer internship where I built automated PySpark and Airflow pipelines. Today, my goal is to build end-to-end solutions—from scalable data infrastructure to intelligent models—that turn raw information into something that actually solves a real problem.
